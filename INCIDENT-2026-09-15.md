# VPS incident: September 15, 2026

Deployment: `/home/ubuntu/growonspot` on the supplied VPS; service `growonspot-trader-1`.

## Findings

- The bot recorded one SELL signal from Telegram message 13402, posted at 13:32:30 IST. OCR extracted entry 4292.13, SL 4307.92 and TP 4257.24.
- The submitted market order filled at 4275.03 for 0.005 XAU, at 5× leverage. The old 0.50% price-gap tolerance accepted this difference. The user subsequently confirmed that entries should remain market orders without this gap filter.
- The exchange reported the same position with SL 4307.92 and TP 4257.24. The database was stuck at SUBMITTED despite the filled order.
- Recovery fetched the entire account order history. There were at least 100 full pages of 100 orders, so it raised a pagination-limit error before using the matching order already returned on page 1.
- The old main loop reconciled before reading Telegram. That error prevented progress past message 13403 and caused repeated unhealthy status.
- Message 13400 at 13:07:55 IST was a photo captioned “Partial book”; it had no edit timestamp. It preceded the recorded bot trade. The other visible “Partial book” text was posted September 14 at 11:43:55 IST. No later matching close text was present in the channel history inspected during this investigation.
- Photo captions were excluded from close handling because photo entry processing ran first. This was a separate real defect even though the inspected caption preceded the current trade.

## Changes

- Stop order lookup immediately when the stored exchange order ID is found.
- Filter position queries by XAUUSDT at the exchange.
- Check closing keywords before image-entry handling, including captions. Exit-caption images never open a position.
- Process Telegram independently of reconciliation failures; preserve pending close intent.
- Keep market entries and remove the entry-gap filter, as requested. Signal field validation, price geometry, one-position isolation, and sizing still apply.
- Log useful API failure endpoint/status information and alert on reconciliation/message errors.

## Validation

- 29 tests passed locally and inside the VPS container, including OCR on both supplied references and the actual incident image.
- On a copy of the production SQLite database, read-only exchange reconciliation recovered the correct OPEN position in 1.7 seconds.
- An isolated replay of a subsequent “Partial book” photo caption requested a full close of the tracked position. Exchange writes were blocked; the exit method was intercepted. No test trade or real exit was placed by this validation.
